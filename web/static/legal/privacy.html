Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

In short: we collect what we need to run the Service: account details, application registrations, and request metadata for traffic through the gateway. We do not store the contents of requests or responses that pass through the gateway. Data is hosted in Sydney, Australia. We don't sell personal information or use advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how RelayOps ("we", "us") handles personal information when you use the RelayOps console, API gateway, developer hub and Orbit AI assistant (the "Service"). We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. What we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Administrator accounts: name, email, team, role, a one-way hash of your password, and sign-in timesTo give you secure access to the console and control what you can do
Developer hub registrations: name, email, application name, the IP address used to register, and your API subscriptionsTo issue and manage API access, and to prevent abuse of self-registration
API keys and tokens: stored only as one-way hashesTo authenticate calls without being able to recover your secrets
Gateway request metadata: time, API, consumer, method, path, status code, latency, response size, client IP address and error text. Only a sample of requests is recorded (currently 5%)To operate, secure and troubleshoot the Service and show you analytics
Audit records: which account made which configuration change, and whenSecurity and accountability
Orbit AI questions: your question and the configuration and metrics needed to answer it, with secrets removedTo answer your question (see section 4)

We do not store the bodies of requests or responses that pass through the gateway. Short-lived counters for rate limits and quotas are kept in memory only.

3. How long we keep it

  • Gateway request metadata: 72 hours, then deleted automatically.
  • Accounts, registrations and configuration: while your account is active, and deleted on request (section 7).
  • Audit records: kept for security and accountability. They can be exported and archived, and are removed only under a documented retention process.
  • Backups: a full encrypted backup is taken daily for disaster recovery and deleted automatically after 30 days. The 7 most recent backups are always kept, even if they are older, so the Service can be restored if backups were interrupted.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the Service. They process information on our behalf and only for that purpose:

ProviderWhat forWhere
Amazon Web ServicesServers that run the Service, and our monitoring alertsSydney, Australia
SupabaseDatabase and encrypted backup storageSydney, Australia
NVIDIAAI model that answers Orbit AI questions, used only when you ask Orbit a questionUnited States

Because Orbit AI uses a provider in the United States, your questions and the related context (with secrets removed) are disclosed overseas when you use Orbit. If you don't want this, don't use Orbit; the rest of the Service works without it.

We don't sell personal information. We will disclose it to others only if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and security of the Service and its users.

5. Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers. The console keeps your sign-in session in your browser's storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

6. Security

Traffic is encrypted with TLS. Passwords, API keys and tokens are stored as one-way hashes, and stored secrets are encrypted. Administrator access is limited by role, changes are audit-logged, and the Service is monitored around the clock. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and data. Email us from the address on your account and we will respond within 30 days. You can also delete applications and API keys yourself in the developer hub.

8. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us first and we will try to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and developers and is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We will post updates here with a new date, and notify you of significant changes by email or in the console.
